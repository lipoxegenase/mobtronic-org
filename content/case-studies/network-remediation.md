# High-Stakes Network Remediation

Following widespread security breaches, major global corporations (including Coca-Cola and Johnson & Johnson) faced critical vulnerabilities and active network threats. Operational integrity was compromised, and immediate recovery and fortification protocols were required.

Our engagement began with a comprehensive operational audit to locate vulnerabilities and isolate active exploit attempts on core networking assets.

<!-- gated -->

### The Technical Solution & Execution

To remediate these critical environments under extreme time pressure, we implemented a multi-phase system restoration protocol:

1. **Vulnerability Isolation:** We deployed custom diagnostic tooling to identify active threat vectors and isolated compromised subnets without causing additional service disruption.
2. **Firmware & Protocol Remediation:** Audited and updated critical hardware firmware across dozens of core switches, firewalls, and routers, closing known zero-day exploits.
3. **Hardened Configuration Policy:** Rebuilt internal routing policy configurations using automated scripts, disabling unsecure legacy protocols (such as obsolete SNMP versions and insecure Telnet management) and enforcing encrypted control plane communication.
4. **Resiliency Testing:** Conducted mock failovers and active stress tests to verify operating integrity post-remediation.

### The Impact
By systematically locking down the network perimeter, we successfully restored operational integrity across global business divisions with zero additional downtime. The modernized hardware configurations now proactively block zero-day vectors, ensuring long-term systemic stability.
