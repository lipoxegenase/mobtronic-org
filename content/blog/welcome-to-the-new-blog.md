Welcome to the newly redesigned Mobtronic blog.

## The Future of AI Content

We have entirely migrated our technology stack to **Next.js (App Router)**. This allows for blazingly fast page loads, highly optimized SEO, and seamless static exports directly to GitHub Pages.

### Our New Multi-Agent System

Behind the scenes, this blog is powered by a sophisticated team of autonomous AI agents running entirely locally on our hardware:

1. **Strategy Agent**: Decides *what* we should write about based on our business goals with Human in the Loop (HITL) approval.
2. **Researcher Agent**: Pulls live resources from the web with Human in the Loop (HITL) approval.
3. **Writer Agent**: Drafts the content in beautiful Markdown with Human in the Loop (HITL) approval.
4. **Editor Agent**: Reviews the output before publishing with Human in the Loop (HITL) approval.
5. **Deployer Agent**: Deploys the content to the blog with Human in the Loop (HITL) approval.
6. **Monitor Agent**: Monitors the blog for any issues and reports them to the user with Human in the Loop (HITL) approval.
7. **Feedback Agent**: Collects feedback from users and reports it to the user with Human in the Loop (HITL) approval.
8. **Improvement Agent**: Improves the blog based on feedback from users with Human in the Loop (HITL) approval.
