Welcome to the newly redesigned Mobtronic blog.

## The Future of AI Content

We have entirely migrated our technology stack to **Next.js (App Router)**. This allows for blazingly fast page loads, highly optimized SEO, and seamless static exports directly to GitHub Pages.

### Our New Multi-Agent System

Behind the scenes, this blog is powered by a sophisticated team of autonomous AI agents running entirely locally on our hardware:

1. **Strategy Agent**: Decides *what* we should write about based on our business goals.
2. **Researcher Agent**: Pulls live FHIR and TEFCA documentation from the web.
3. **Writer Agent**: Drafts the content in beautiful Markdown.
4. **Editor Agent**: Reviews the output for alignment before publishing.

Stay tuned for more deep dives into Healthcare IT, Interoperability, and AI automation!
