Renewable Energy's Role in Climate Change Mitigation: A Review

October 15, 2023

Introduction

Climate change remains one of the most pressing global challenges, with rising carbon emissions driving temperature increases and ecological disruptions 1. Renewable energy technologies offer a transformative solution to reduce dependency on fossil fuels and mitigate environmental degradation.

Solar Energy Advancements

Solar photovoltaic (PV) systems have seen a 90% cost reduction since 2010, making them a cornerstone of renewable energy 2. Modern perovskite solar cells now achieve efficiency rates exceeding 30%, surpassing traditional silicon-based technologies 3.

Wind Energy Potential

Offshore wind farms generate up to 80 GW of capacity worldwide, with projections indicating a 200% growth by 2030 4. Innovations in floating turbine technology enable energy production in deeper waters, accessing stronger wind resources 5.

Challenges and Opportunities

Despite progress, renewable energy storage and grid integration remain critical barriers. Battery energy storage system costs have fallen by 89% since 2010 6, yet further innovation is required to match demand-side fluctuations. Policy incentives and public-private partnerships are essential for widespread adoption 7.
